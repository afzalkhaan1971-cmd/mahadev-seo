Unexpected security alert

Cricket ID OTP not requested? Take these 7 safety steps

An unexpected OTP does not always mean the account was hacked, but it can signal a login or password-reset attempt. Do not share the code and check the account through a verified route.

Quick answer: do not reply, click a message link or give the OTP to anyone. Open the genuine website independently, secure the associated email, review account activity and change reused passwords.

Why did you receive an OTP you did not request?

Someone may have entered your phone number or email by mistake. It may also be an automated login attempt, a password-reset attempt or the first part of an impersonation scam. The code usually expires quickly, but a caller or message asking you to reveal it is a serious warning sign.

Do not assume that a familiar logo, sender name or WhatsApp profile proves the message is genuine. Never use a link inside an unexpected message to check the account.

7 steps to protect the account

  1. Keep the OTP private. Do not read it aloud, forward it, screenshot it or enter it on a page opened by someone else.
  2. Ignore message links. Type the previously verified website address yourself or use the genuine app.
  3. Review recent activity. Check login alerts, active sessions, profile changes and transactions you did not authorise.
  4. Secure the associated email. Use a unique password, enable multi-factor authentication and review recovery details.
  5. Change reused passwords. Replace an exposed or reused password through the verified recovery page.
  6. Sign out unknown sessions. Remove unfamiliar devices or connections when session controls are available.
  7. Preserve evidence. Save the message, sender details, time, website address and related alerts.

Should you reset the password immediately?

Reset it if the password is weak, reused, exposed, or if the account shows unknown activity. Start only from the genuine website. Our safe Cricket ID password-reset guide explains the process and common OTP delivery problems.

If recovery details changed or you cannot sign in, follow the hacked Cricket ID recovery checklist instead of repeatedly guessing passwords.

What if you already shared or entered the OTP?

Treat the account as exposed. From a trusted device, secure the associated email first, change the affected password, sign out other sessions and review recovery details. Check for unauthorised activity and contact verified support independently. Use our detailed guide on what to do after sharing a Cricket ID OTP.

When should you report the incident?

Contact the platform through contact details published on its verified website if account access, recovery details or transactions changed. If money is involved, contact the bank or payment provider immediately through its official channel.

For cyber financial fraud in India, call 1930 promptly and use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Keep transaction references and original messages available.

Quick questions

Can someone access the account with only the Cricket ID?

An ID identifies the account but should not authenticate access by itself. Passwords, PINs, OTPs and recovery links must remain private.

Can genuine support ask for the OTP?

Do not send an OTP, password or recovery link to another person. Enter a code only on the genuine page when you initiated the action.

What if several OTPs arrive?

Do not use any of them. Review the account and associated email through verified channels, then change exposed or reused passwords.
