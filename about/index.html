About this website

Independent information, clearly explained.

Mahadev is an educational resource designed to make Cricket ID terminology, account safety, privacy and India-specific research easier to understand.

Our purpose

We publish plain-language guides for readers researching Cricket ID topics. Our aim is to separate useful facts from unsupported promises and help readers ask better questions before using any third-party platform.

Editorial principles

  • Explain terminology without unnecessary jargon
  • Encourage independent verification of platforms and claims
  • Distinguish educational information from legal or financial advice
  • Promote account security, privacy and responsible decision-making
  • Correct material errors when they are identified

Independence

This website is not a cricket authority, betting operator, account provider or representative of any third-party platform. References to general terms are provided for educational context.
